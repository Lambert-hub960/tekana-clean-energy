Section 01

Executive Profile & One Health Systems Methodology

Jean Lambert Tuyishimire is an agricultural mechanization engineer dedicated to developing decentralized, low-cost technologies that intercept waste streams, protect natural habitats, and enhance community nutrition. Operating under a One Health framework, his work addresses the interdependencies between environmental conservation, agricultural productivity, and human health.

Forest Ecosystem Conservation

Replacing wood fuel with recycled bio-briquettes eliminates deforestation pressure along sensitive forest buffer zones.

Household Clean Air & Health

Smokeless bio-briquette combustion eliminates toxic particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$) and carbon monoxide ($\text{CO}$) in household kitchens.

Soil Organic Health

Returning spent bio-compost and bio-char back to farming fields restores depleted organic carbon and vital soil micro-flora.

Socio-Economic Inclusion

Empowering women produce vendors and youth through localized equipment operation, green micro-enterprises, and cost reductions.

Section 02 — Flagship Venture

Tekana Clean Energy: Circular Bio-Waste Valorization

The Ecosystem Challenge in Rwanda

Over 80% of households in Rwanda rely on wood fuel and traditional charcoal for daily cooking, accelerating forest clearance along wildlife corridors and protected parks. Simultaneously, over 1.2 million metric tons of unutilized maize cobs and husks accumulate annually as agricultural waste or are burned openly. Indoor smoke from traditional cooking causes over 4,500 premature deaths each year, primarily affecting rural women and children.

The 5-Stage Circular Manufacturing Process

01

Raw Agro-Waste Sourcing

Sourcing maize cobs, crop husks, and agricultural residues directly from farming cooperatives and market centers.

Input: Crop Residues
02

Carbonization & Dust Processing

Subjecting biomass to controlled pyrolysis and blending with recycled market charcoal dust (60:40 ratio).

Fine Charcoal Feedstock
03

Bio-Organic Starch Binding

Preparing a non-toxic cassava starch slurry (5–8% concentration) that binds particles with zero chemical fumes.

Cassava Binder Slurry
04

High-Pressure Mechanical Pressing

Compacting using dual-piston steel lever presses (12–15 MPa) forming cylinders with a 15mm airflow chimney.

12–15 MPa Compaction
05

Solar Curing & Clean Bio-Fuel

Passive solar curing racks drying briquettes to <10% moisture, delivering high calorific, smokeless fuel.

24–26 MJ/kg Fuel Output

Section 03

Complementary AgTech Pillars

FruiFresh Rwanda

Off-grid evaporative cooling units constructed with charcoal double timber walls and low-wattage solar drip pumps. Integrated with drone aerial scouting (RGB and multispectral imaging) to monitor crop stress and schedule harvest timing.

• 8–12°C internal temperature drop
• Yields increased from 4.0 to 4.7 t/ha

Agri-Smart Initiative

Passive solar cabinet dehydrators utilizing thermal absorption glass, food-grade aluminum frames, and natural convective airflow. Extends perishable produce shelf-life without electricity or chemical preservatives.

• Shelf life extended from 2 days to 6+ months
• 90% financial loss reduction for market women

VitaHarvest Flour (KuraNeza)

Micro-milling venture producing **KuraNeza**—a bio-fortified composite flour blending maize, sorghum, and sun-dried oyster mushroom powder (*Pleurotus ostreatus*). Converts spent mushroom substrate into enriched bio-compost for soil health.

• 50% lower cost than commercial flours
• 20–30% cereal yield increase via compost
